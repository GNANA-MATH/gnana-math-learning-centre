Medicine Safety Centre

Practical safety reminders for responsible medicine use.

Before taking a medicine

  • Confirm generic name, brand, strength, and dosage form.
  • Read the current leaflet and follow prescriber instructions.
  • Check duplicate active ingredients across combination products.
  • Tell your clinician about allergies, pregnancy, breastfeeding, and other medicines.
  • Ask about kidney/liver conditions and interactions.
  • Check expiry date and product-specific storage.

Important cautions

  • Do not start, stop, or change a prescribed medicine without professional advice.
  • Do not use this demo to determine a child's dose.
  • Do not share prescription medicines.
  • Antibiotics require clinician oversight; do not self-medicate.
  • This information is not exhaustive or universal across brands and countries.

Urgent symptoms

Seek urgent medical help for difficulty breathing, facial/throat swelling, collapse or loss of consciousness, severe allergic reaction, suspected overdose, or serious adverse reaction.

For suspected poisoning or overdose, contact local emergency services or an appropriate poison information service immediately. Do not induce vomiting or wait for this application.
