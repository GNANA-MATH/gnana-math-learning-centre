Important tax-year selector: FY 2025–26 is governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961 and is reported as AY 2026–27. Income earned from 1 April 2026 is Tax Year 2026–27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Do not mix the two frameworks.

1. Taxpayer and tax period

Enter income after applying the appropriate income-head rules, except salary/pension, where the standard deduction is applied by this estimator. Do not enter capital gains or other special-rate income here.

2. Deductions for old-regime estimate

Enter only amounts you believe are eligible. The tool applies common caps, but does not establish legal eligibility.

3. Estimated tax comparison

Choose “Calculate and compare” to generate an estimate.

4. Pensioner guidance

  • Government and private pension may be taxed under different rules depending on the pension type.
  • For this estimator, regular taxable pension is entered with salary. The applicable standard deduction is then estimated.
  • Bank-interest deductions for senior citizens are not automatically added; check the conditions and applicable regime before claiming them.
  • Family pension is not identical to regular pension. It may require separate treatment and should not be included as salary without checking.
If you receive family pension, commuted pension, arrears, foreign pension, or pension plus capital gains/business income, obtain a case-specific calculation before relying on the estimate.

5. ITR preparation checklist

  • PAN and Aadhaar details, as applicable
  • Form 16 / pension certificate and salary or pension statements
  • Annual Information Statement (AIS), Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS), and Form 26AS
  • Bank interest certificates and bank account details
  • Proof for deductions and eligible investments
  • Rental income, property-loan interest, capital gains, and other income records
  • Previous return and tax-payment challans, if relevant

This is a preparation checklist, not an ITR filing system. It does not submit a return or log in to your tax account.
